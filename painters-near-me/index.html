Questions

Service area questions

Which cities does Painter Hotline cover?

47 cities and towns on Colorado's Front Range: the Denver metro, south to Castle Rock, north to Longmont, east along I-70 to about Deer Trail and along I-76 to Fort Morgan, and west on I-70 to Breckenridge.

Do you charge extra to travel to the mountains or the plains?

Travel is built into the written estimate rather than added later, and we group jobs in the same area into single trips. Mountain and plains work is planned further ahead.

Why do you list the age of homes for each city?

Because the age of a house changes the job more than its size does. Homes built before 1978 may have lead-based paint, homes from the 1970s through the 1990s often have hardboard siding, and newer homes usually need caulk and trim work on their first repaint.

What if my town is not listed?

Call with the address. If it is inside the area above we will quote it; if it is not, we will say so.
