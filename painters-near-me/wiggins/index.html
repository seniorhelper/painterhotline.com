Straight answers

Painting questions from Wiggins homeowners

Will you tell me if my trim needs replacing first?

Yes. If wood is soft or rotted, we say so before quoting coatings, because painting failing wood is money wasted.

How do I get a painting quote from the hotline in Wiggins?

Call 720-208-5645 or send the quote form on any page. Tell us the city, what needs painting and your timeline, and add photos if you have them. Quotes are free, written and carry no obligation.

Are you insured?

Yes, fully insured, and we provide a certificate of insurance with every quote. Colorado issues no statewide painting license, so insurance and a written scope are the two documents worth asking any painter to produce.

What warranty comes with the work?

A 5-year workmanship warranty, in writing, covering our preparation and application. The coating itself also carries its manufacturer warranty from Sherwin-Williams, PPG or Behr.
