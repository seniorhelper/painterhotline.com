Know Your Climate

What Every Colorado
Homeowner Should Know.

Denver's environment is harder on paint than almost anywhere else in the country. Understanding why helps you make smarter decisions about materials, timing, and maintenance.

☀️ 300+ Days of Sun — And UV Is Brutal at Altitude

Denver sits at 5,280 feet above sea level. At that elevation, UV radiation is roughly 25% more intense than at sea level. Exterior paint fades and degrades significantly faster here than in lower-elevation cities. This is why we always recommend 100% acrylic exterior paints with UV-resistant formulas. They cost more upfront. They last two to four years longer. That math always works out in your favor.

❄️ Freeze-Thaw Cycles Crack Inferior Paint

Denver temperatures can swing 40 or 50 degrees in a single day. That kind of thermal movement causes wood to expand and contract continuously throughout winter and spring. Paint that lacks the elasticity to handle it will crack, peel, and fail early. Quality elastomeric and acrylic paints flex with your home's movement. Cheap paint does not — and you will be repainting far sooner than you planned.

💧 Low Humidity Speeds Drying — And Fading

Colorado's famously dry air is a double-edged sword for painters. On one hand, low humidity means faster dry times and fewer weather delays. On the other hand, dry conditions accelerate moisture loss from paint films, which can cause brittleness over time. Using paints formulated for arid climates — and maintaining exterior surfaces with periodic cleaning — extends their life significantly.

🌨️ Hail Damage Means More Than Dents

Colorado sees some of the highest hail frequency in the country. Hailstorms that damage roofing and siding also impact exterior paint — creating micro-abrasions that accelerate weathering and expose wood to moisture infiltration. After a significant hail event, it is worth having your exterior paint professionally inspected. Catching small failures early prevents expensive rot and structural repairs later.

🏠 When to Repaint Your Denver Home

Most Denver homes need exterior repainting every five to seven years — compared to eight to ten years in gentler climates. Interior paint in well-maintained homes lasts considerably longer: five to ten years in living areas, three to five in high-traffic zones like kitchens and entryways. Fading, chalking, peeling, and cracking are all signs it is time. Do not wait until you see bare wood — by then, prep costs have increased significantly.

🎨 Choosing Colors for Colorado Light

High-altitude light is different. Colors that look muted in a showroom can appear dramatically brighter on a sun-drenched Colorado wall. Cool grays can shift slightly blue. Warm whites can look almost golden. Always test paint samples on your actual exterior surface and observe them at multiple times of day before committing. Resources from Behr and Sherwin-Williams can help narrow your options.

Interior Painting in Denver Homes

Interior painting is one of the fastest and most cost-effective ways to transform a home. A fresh coat of paint in the right color can make a small room feel larger, a dark space feel brighter, and an outdated home feel completely refreshed. However, the results are only as good as the preparation that precedes them.

Before any interior paint is applied on a Painter Hotline project, our team washes the walls, fills any holes or cracks with the appropriate filler, sands rough areas smooth, applies painter's tape with precision, and lays drop cloths on every surface. We protect your trim and switch plates. We move furniture away from walls carefully. We take the time that most painters skip. Consequently, our finishes look cleaner and last longer.

For interior projects, we typically recommend paints with higher sheens in areas that see moisture or frequent cleaning — kitchens, bathrooms, laundry rooms, and children's rooms. Eggshell and satin finishes work beautifully in living areas and bedrooms. Flat finishes can look stunning on ceilings. We are happy to walk you through the best options for every room in your home.

Exterior Painting and Colorado's Climate

Exterior painting in Denver is genuinely different from exterior painting almost anywhere else in the country. The combination of 300-plus sunny days, intense UV radiation at altitude, dramatic temperature swings, and frequent hail events creates conditions that challenge even the best paint products. This is why material selection and surface preparation are especially critical here.

We always start exterior projects with a thorough pressure washing to remove dirt, mildew, and chalking from old paint. After that, we scrape any loose or peeling areas and sand the edges smooth so new paint feathers in correctly. We caulk every gap around windows, doors, trim, and penetrations — because gaps are where water gets in, and water is the enemy of any paint job. We prime bare wood and previously stained surfaces. Only then do we apply paint.

For exterior surfaces, we work exclusively with 100% acrylic paints rated for high-UV environments. Brands like Sherwin-Williams Duration, Behr Marquee Exterior, and Benjamin Moore Aura Exterior are among our most recommended products. They cost more per gallon — and they last significantly longer in Colorado conditions, which means better value over the life of your home.

Cabinet Painting — The Smart Kitchen Upgrade

Kitchen cabinet replacement is one of the most expensive home improvement projects a homeowner can undertake. Costs routinely range from $15,000 to $30,000 or more for a standard kitchen, depending on materials and layout. Cabinet painting delivers a stunning visual transformation at a fraction of that cost — typically between $1,200 and $4,500 for a full kitchen.

The key is doing it correctly. Cabinet painting is a meticulous process. It requires thorough degreasing and cleaning, careful sanding to create the right surface profile, a bonding primer that adheres to the existing finish, and a durable topcoat applied in multiple thin layers. Shortcuts at any stage result in peeling, chipping, or an uneven finish that looks worse than the original cabinets.

Our cabinet painting process produces a factory-quality finish. We remove all doors and hardware, paint them in a controlled environment, and reinstall them after the finish has fully cured. The result is smooth, durable, and beautiful — without the cost or waste of full replacement.

Deck Staining in Colorado — Why It Matters More Here

Colorado's combination of intense UV, low humidity, and freeze-thaw cycles makes deck staining one of the most important maintenance tasks a homeowner can perform. Unprotected wood degrades rapidly in Colorado conditions. UV causes graying and surface checking. Moisture infiltration causes swelling, cracking, and eventually rot. A quality penetrating stain applied correctly can protect your deck for three to five years — and make it look beautiful throughout.

We use penetrating oil-based stains from brands like TWP (Total Wood Preservative) and Armstrong Clark — two of the most respected deck stain manufacturers in the industry. These products absorb into the wood fiber rather than forming a surface film, which means they flex with the wood's natural movement and resist peeling far better than film-forming products. Therefore, they typically outlast cheaper box-store stains by two to three years.
