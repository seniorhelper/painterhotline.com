Where the money goes

Ten things that move a Colorado painting price

1

Surface condition

The largest swing on exteriors. Peeling and chalking add hours of scraping, sanding and priming before any finish coat.

2

Height and access

Two-story walls, walkouts, steep mountain lots and tight urban setbacks all change staging and pace.

3

Substrate

Wood, hardboard, fiber cement, stucco, brick, log and vinyl each need different prep and products.

4

Trim detail

Older homes in Denver, Longmont and Brighton carry far more trim per wall, and trim is slow, precise work.

5

Coats and color

Deep color changes and porous surfaces often need an extra coat or a tinted primer.

6

Product grade

Top-tier exterior lines cost more per gallon and generally buy years of extra service life at altitude.

7

Home age

Pre-1978 homes may require federally mandated lead-safe work practices, which add containment and cleanup.

8

HOA requirements

Color approvals affect scheduling more than price, though both move when a submission is late.

9

Season and travel

Exterior weather windows are limited, mountain seasons shorter, and rush scheduling costs more than flexibility.

10

Occupancy

Working around furniture, pets, tenants or business hours takes longer than an empty building.

Pricing questions

How much does it cost to paint a house in Colorado?

Published 2026 Front Range data puts exterior house painting at roughly $1.55 to $4.10 per square foot of paintable surface, and interior painting at about $1.50 to $3.50 per square foot. Condition, height, trim detail and product choice decide where a project lands.

Why do painting quotes differ so much?

Because they describe different work. Preparation, primer, coat count, product line and warranty are the four places a lower number usually comes from. Compare those lines before comparing totals.

Does the hotline charge for quotes?

No. Quotes are free and written, with no obligation, anywhere we work.

Can you quote from photos?

Often, yes. Clear photos of each elevation or room, plus the age of the home and anything that is failing, is usually enough for a written number. Peeling, water damage and difficult access get a site visit instead.

Do mountain towns cost more?

Sometimes. Travel, a shorter weather window and tougher exposure can affect scheduling and product selection in Evergreen, Idaho Springs, Georgetown and Summit County. Your written quote spells it out rather than hiding it in a day rate.

How does the 25% discount work?

Projects booked by October 31, 2026 receive 25% off labor. Paint and materials are not included in the discount, and it is applied to your written quote so you can see the difference.
