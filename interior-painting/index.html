When it is time

Signs a room is overdue

Interior paint rarely fails dramatically. It just quietly makes the whole house feel tired.

Scuffs that will not wash off

Hallways, stairwells and kids' rooms wear through flat paint faster than any other surface in the house.

Nail pops and seam cracks

Colorado's dry air and seasonal movement open drywall seams and push fasteners out.

Ceiling stains

Old water or smoke staining needs a stain-blocking primer, or it bleeds straight back through fresh paint.

Dated color

A color from two owners ago drags down finishes you actually like.

Just moved in

Fresh neutral walls are the fastest way to make a place feel like yours.

Yellowed trim

Trim takes the most contact in the house, and tired trim undermines brand-new wall color.

In the scope

What an interior project includes

  • Full protection. Floors, furniture, fixtures and hardware covered or removed before a lid comes off.
  • Real patching. Nail pops, anchor holes, cracks and dings filled, sanded smooth and spot-primed.
  • Caulked trim lines. Gaps at baseboards and casing sealed so the finish line reads sharp.
  • Stain blocking. Water, smoke, tannin and marker stains primed so nothing bleeds through.
  • Hand-cut lines. Crisp edges where walls meet ceilings, trim and corners.
  • Daily cleanup. You still live there, so the site gets cleared every evening.
  • Walkthrough first. You inspect every room before we call it finished.

Straight answers

Interior painting questions

How much does interior painting cost?

Interior painting is commonly quoted between $1.50 and $3.50 per square foot in this market, with many projects priced per room. Ceiling height, repairs, trim, color change and product line move the number most.

Can you paint in winter?

Yes. Interior work runs year-round because it happens inside a heated home. Winter is the easiest time of year to get on the schedule.

How long does a room take?

A standard single room is often a one-day job. A full interior usually runs several days depending on size, ceilings, trim and repairs.

What sheen should each room get?

Commonly flat on ceilings, matte or eggshell in living areas and bedrooms, satin in kitchens, baths and kids' rooms, and semi-gloss on trim and doors.

Is the paint safe around kids and pets?

Most interior paints today are water-based and low in VOC, and Colorado tightened VOC limits for architectural coatings in 2020. Zero-VOC options are available on request.

Do I need to move everything out?

No. Clearing small items and art helps and can lower cost, and we cover and move larger pieces as part of protection.

Can you fix a ceiling water stain?

Once the leak is resolved, yes. The stain needs a stain-blocking primer first or it bleeds back through.

Do you paint basements?

Yes, including drywall repair, stain blocking on masonry walls and durable finishes for high-use spaces.
