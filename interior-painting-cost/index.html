Cost factors

What moves an interior painting price

  • Room size. More floor area means more wall area to prep and paint.
  • Wall height. Taller walls mean more square feet of wall, and vaulted ceilings need ladders or planks.
  • What is included. Walls only, or walls plus ceilings, baseboards, door and window trim.
  • Wall condition. Nail pops, cracks, dents, texture matching and wallpaper removal are labor before any paint goes on.
  • Color change. Covering a dark or bold color often needs a primer or an extra coat.
  • Home age. Pre-1978 homes may need lead-safe work practices; see older homes.

Questions

Interior cost questions

How much does it cost to paint the interior of a house in Denver?

With Painter Hotline, a typical medium bedroom (about 12 by 12 feet, 8-foot ceilings) runs about $1,150 to $1,275, and a 16 by 20 living or family room about $1,975 to $2,175. Our unit prices are $2.05 to $2.25 per square foot of wall, $1.60 to $1.80 per square foot of flat ceiling and $3.35 to $3.45 per linear foot of baseboard, with an $870 minimum for interior jobs.

What makes interior painting cost more?

Bigger rooms, tall or vaulted ceilings, painting ceilings and trim as well as walls, repairs and texture matching, and big color changes that need primer or an extra coat. Furniture moving and access also add time.

Is painting the ceiling extra?

Yes. Ceilings are priced separately from walls because they take their own prep, product and setup. In the calculator, tick Ceiling for each room you want included.

Does a dark-to-light color change cost more?

Yes. Covering a dark or bold color usually takes an extra coat. We price that at $0.90 to $1.05 per square foot of wall, and the calculator adds it when you choose a big color change.

Does an older home cost more to paint?

If the home was built before 1978 and painted surfaces will be disturbed, federal lead-safe work practices apply. We price those at $380 per interior work area.

Is the calculator price a quote?

No. It is a typical Painter Hotline price range so you can budget. The final price comes in writing after a free estimate from photos or in person, and it names every surface, the prep and the products.

Is there a discount right now?

Yes. Painting projects booked by December 31, 2026 get 25% off the labor. Paint and materials are not included, and the calculator range does not include the discount.
