What we are

A Colorado painting company with six crews, each doing one kind of work. Over 20 years of painting in this state, most of it along the Front Range, and a structure built around the idea that specialization produces better work than versatility does.

What we believe, in practice

  • Preparation is the product. Anyone can apply color. Whether it lasts eight years or three is decided before the finish coat opens.
  • Say the hard thing early. If your siding needs carpentry before paint, you hear it at the estimate, not after you have paid for coatings that were going to fail.
  • Write it down. Surfaces, preparation by surface, product line, sheen, coat count, schedule, terms, warranty. If it matters, it is on the page.
  • Answer the phone. During the project and after the invoice clears. Same number either way.

People first, then profits

That order matters and it is not decoration. A painting company that optimizes for margin first starts skipping the wash, thinning the coats and quoting "prep as needed." It works for about two years, and then the referrals stop.

We would rather be the company you call the next time something needs coating, and the one you give your neighbor's number to. That only happens if the first project was handled properly and somebody picked up when you called afterward.

We are not perfect

Every painting company has a day that goes sideways on a long enough timeline. A board worse underneath than it looked. A color that reads differently on the wall than on the chip. Weather that moves a schedule.

What we control is being reachable when that happens and fixing it without an argument. Attention to detail and the kind of family-friendly service we would want in our own homes is in the mission, and it is what we actually get judged on.

How a project runs

  1. You send photos or call. Tell us what is bothering you and the year the building went up.
  2. The right crew lead scopes it. Not a general inbox. The person who runs that type of work.
  3. Written estimate within 24 hours. Everything named, including any repair counted individually.
  4. Colors and approvals. Samples on your actual surfaces, and any HOA submission prepared before scheduling.
  5. Protect and prepare. The stage that decides the outcome.
  6. Coat it. Inside the manufacturer's conditions, sequenced around sun and wind, cleaned up daily.
  7. You walk it. We fix anything you flag before the balance. Then five years of written coverage.

Questions

About us

How long have you been painting in Colorado?

Over 20 years, most of it along the Front Range.

Are you licensed?

Colorado does not issue a statewide painting license. What matters is insurance, which we carry and provide a certificate for at the estimate, and a written scope you can hold us to.

Do you use subcontractors?

Work is performed by experienced painters who do this full time, assigned by crew, and the person who scoped your project is accountable for how it turns out.

What areas do you cover?

Colorado's Front Range and the corridors out of it, from Castle Rock to Longmont, west into Summit County and east along I-70 to about Deer Trail.

Who do I call if something goes wrong?

The same number you booked with: 720-208-5645. That does not change after the invoice.

Why six crews instead of one?

Because interior, exterior, cabinets, commercial, decks and specialty coatings are genuinely different trades. A crew that does one every day gets measurably better at it.
