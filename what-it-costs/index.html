Direct answer

On Colorado's Front Range, published 2026 cost data puts exterior painting at about $1.55 to $4.10 per square foot of paintable surface, interior painting at about $2 to $6 per square foot, and kitchen cabinet painting at $30 to $60 per linear foot (a full kitchen usually lands between $2,000 and $8,000) (against $15,000 to $30,000 or more to replace the cabinets). Where a project lands inside those ranges depends mostly on the condition of what is there now.

Paying for a paint job twice

Here is an illustration, not a quote. A cheap bid skips washing, scraping and primer and looks fine for a season or two. Then it starts to peel, because it was never really stuck to anything. The next painter has to take that failed coat off before doing the job properly. You end up paying for the cheap job, the removal and the proper job, which is why our estimates list the preparation surface by surface instead of saying "prep as needed."

Ways to spend less without cutting prep

  • Schedule interiors in winter. November through March is the easiest time to get the dates you want.
  • Split the exterior by side. South and west walls fail first; do them now and the rest next year.
  • Keep it to one trip. Doing the whole exterior at once avoids paying for setup twice.
  • Move furniture and clear shelves before an interior crew arrives.
  • Re-aim the sprinklers. Water hitting the bottom of a wall ruins paint there every year.
  • Team up with a neighbor. Two houses on one street in one trip saves on both.

Questions

Cost questions

What does it cost to paint the outside of a house in Colorado?

Angi’s 2026 Denver cost data puts exterior painting at about $1.55 to $4.10 per square foot of paintable wall surface. A ranch with 1,800 square feet of siding and trim would fall somewhere between roughly $2,800 and $7,400 on those numbers; the condition of the old paint decides where in that span it lands.

Is paintable surface the same as the size of my house?

No. Floor area tells you little about an exterior. Walk the outside: the perimeter in feet times the wall height (about 9 feet per story) gives a rough wall area, and gables add more. Interiors are usually priced from the floor area of the rooms being painted.

Why do two painting bids differ by thousands?

Line them up on four things: the preparation listed, the exact product and sheen, how many coats, and the written warranty. The lower number is almost always smaller on one of those four.

Are estimates free?

Yes, in person or from photos, with no obligation. We do not follow up with a sales sequence.

How does payment work?

A deposit holds your start date and the balance is due when the work is finished and you have walked it with us. The terms are printed on the estimate.

Does the 25% October discount apply to materials?

No. It applies to labor on projects booked by November 15, 2026. Paint and materials are billed at the normal rate.
