Process

What makes cabinet painting last

  1. Degrease. Kitchen cabinets carry years of cooking residue, and primer will not bond through it.
  2. Scuff sand. Factory finishes are slick on purpose; sanding gives the primer something to grip.
  3. Bonding primer. A primer made to stick to cured factory finishes, not ordinary wall primer.
  4. Sprayed cabinet enamel. Doors and drawer fronts sprayed off the boxes, boxes sprayed in place behind plastic, for a smooth, hard finish.

Cabinet questions

How much does it cost to paint kitchen cabinets in Denver?

With Painter Hotline, spraying kitchen cabinets runs $175 to $195 per door, $110 to $120 per drawer front and $75 to $80 per linear foot for the boxes and face frames, so a kitchen with 22 doors, 8 drawers and 24 feet of cabinets comes to about $6,530 to $7,170. Replacing with new installed cabinets typically costs $2,003 to $11,053 according to HomeAdvisor, before countertops.

Is painting cabinets cheaper than replacing them?

Usually by a wide margin. Painting keeps your boxes, doors and countertops; replacing means new boxes, doors and installation, and it often brings new countertops with it.

What is the difference between cabinet painting and refinishing?

People use the words loosely. Painting covers the wood with an opaque sprayed color. Refinishing in the narrow sense keeps a wood look: the cabinets are cleaned, scuffed and given a fresh clear coat. Our cabinet crew does both.

How long is my kitchen out of service?

Our cabinet crew is usually on site three to five days. You keep your countertops and appliances; doors and drawer fronts are off for most of that time.

Can oak cabinets be painted?

Yes. Oak grain shows through paint unless it is filled. You can keep the texture or have the grain filled for a smooth finish, which takes extra labor. We can sample both on one of your doors.
