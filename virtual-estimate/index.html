How it works

What happens after you hit send

  1. We read it the same day. Your photos go to the crew lead for that type of work, not to a general inbox.
  2. We measure what we can see. Photos plus the year built and your answers are enough to price most interiors and straightforward exteriors accurately.
  3. You get it in writing within one business day. Surfaces, preparation itemized by surface, the exact product line and sheen, coat counts, schedule, payment terms and warranty.
  4. If photos are not enough, we say so. Significant peeling, suspected water damage, stucco movement and commercial scopes get a walkthrough, because guessing on those would be dishonest.
  5. Then nothing, unless you want it. No follow-up sequence, no drip emails. You have the number when you are ready.
Questions

About virtual estimates

How accurate is a virtual painting estimate?

For interiors and straightforward exteriors with good photos, close enough to rely on. Condition drives price more than square footage, and condition is visible in a photo. Significant peeling, suspected water damage, stucco movement or a commercial scope needs a walkthrough, and we will tell you rather than guess.

How long does it take to get the estimate back?

Within one business day, usually sooner. That is a written estimate, not a phone call asking to schedule a visit.

What photos should I send?

Stand far enough back to get a whole wall or room in one frame, one per surface. Then close-ups of anything that worries you: peeling, cracking, water staining, damaged trim. For interiors, one shot per room from the doorway works well.

Do you need to come out at all?

Not for most projects. If the photos and your answers are enough, we price it and send it. If they are not, we will say so and arrange a visit.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes, and there is no obligation. We also do not run a follow-up sequence. If you want to compare it against other bids, take your time.

What if I do not know the answers to the questions?

Pick the closest option or skip it. The photos carry most of the information, and the notes box is there for anything that does not fit a dropdown.

Will my photos be used anywhere?

They are used to prepare your estimate and for project documentation. We do not publish customer photos without asking first.
