- Minor-repair exclusion (loaded): 6 sq ft or less per room inside, 20 sq ft or less outside. Penalty amount UNVERIFIED (2018 EPA slide said up to $38,892; outdated, do not publish).
- Colorado is not on EPA's list of states running their own RRP program, so EPA administers it here.
